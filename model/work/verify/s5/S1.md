Level inversion: the level law is monotone in a; the levels are pairwise distinct only for a <= 0x23F (not "~0x300":
at k = 9, M 126 and 125 collide).  In these runs the ambiguity touches only the key-on level (496 <-> a 0x27F..0x282,
taken as the key-on load 0x280) and the first inc-1 attack step (816 <-> a 0x257..0x258); from the second step on a is
unique (0x231, 0x20D, 0x1EC, ...).  Step samples were defined as level changes, which the monotone law makes
equivalent to attenuation changes in the distinct range.

## Reading

The ring lock itself — clock parity and a counter that is a fixed offset from MDEC_CT/2 across runs captured at
unrelated ring positions — is confirmed independently.  The number "K = 6491" is bound to the -1 offset convention for
R < 48 rows: on these AEG-only runs the pair (offset -1, K 6491) is indistinguishable from (offset 0, K 6490), which
HANDOVER already notes (S4: the AEG shares the offset only through the joint fit with the FEG).  Nothing in these
three runs contradicts any part of S1.
